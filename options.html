Date type
CSV format: name,type,month,day,note,recurrence,occurrence,year,regions — type is one of birthday, anniversary, festival, national, remembrance, custom; recurrence is fixed or lunar. The last columns are optional; regions is pipe-separated country codes (e.g. IN|US) or GLOBAL — leave it empty to always show the row. Import adds rows to whichever section they belong in automatically.
